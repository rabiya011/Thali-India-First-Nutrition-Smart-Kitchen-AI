# 🍽️ THALI – AI Powered Nutrition & Smart Kitchen Platform

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

India is facing a silent nutrition crisis.

- Over **77 million diabetics**
- More than **200 million fitness-conscious users**
- Millions managing **PCOS, obesity, heart disease, and thyroid issues**

Yet most Indians make food decisions blindly.

Existing calorie tracking apps are built for Western diets and fail to recognize Indian meals like:
- Dal Tadka  
- Roti  
- Idli  
- Paneer Sabzi  
- Rajma Chawal  
- Homemade Indian food  

Since nearly **70% of Indian meals are home-cooked**, users are forced to guess calories or manually log meals.

Dieticians cost **₹3000–₹5000/month**, making expert guidance inaccessible.

Another common problem:
> People see ingredients in their fridge but don’t know what to cook based on their health goals.

Modern users (especially Gen Z) abandon apps because they are:
- Slow  
- Repetitive  
- Clinical  
- Boring  

---

## 💡 Solution

**THALI** is an AI-powered nutrition and smart kitchen platform built specifically for India.

### 📸 Snap Food → Get Nutrition Instantly
- Upload meal image  
- Get calories, protein, carbs, fats  
- Portion estimation  

### 🥦 Snap Ingredients → Get Smart Recipes
- Capture fridge ingredients  
- Get recipes based on:
  - Weight loss  
  - Diabetes control  
  - Muscle gain  
  - PCOS-friendly diet  
  - Heart health  

### 🎯 Personalized Goals
- Set daily health goals  
- AI adapts recommendations  

### 🔥 Streaks & Gamification
- Daily streaks  
- Milestones  
- Progress badges  

### 📲 Shareable Progress Cards
- Auto-generated Instagram-ready cards

## ⚙️ How It Works

### Flow 1: Snap Thali → Nutrition
1. User uploads food image  
2. AI detects food items  
3. Matches with nutrition database  
4. Displays calories & macros  
5. Saves to profile  

---

### Flow 2: Snap Ingredients → Recipes
1. User uploads fridge image  
2. Ingredients detected  
3. Recipes generated  
4. Filtered by health goals  
5. User cooks healthier meals  

---

### Flow 3: Goal + Streak Engine
1. Meals tracked daily  
2. Goals evaluated automatically  
3. Streak updated  
4. Reward card generated
